What this review tool can tell you

Across __THIRD_FAILED_PLAYS__ eligible failed third-down plays, __THIRD_QUALIFYING_PLAYS__ have at least one qualifying receiver. This offline report includes __THIRD_QUEUE_PLAYS__ sampled plays and __REPLAY_COUNT__ receiver cues. The featured Schwartz play is a deliberately selected illustration; the other plays use a fixed random seed.

Screening rule: nearest defender >2 yards away for ≥0.5 seconds while at/beyond the first-down line and ≥2 yards downfield, from 1 second after the snap through 0.3 seconds before the throw. The first qualifying interval supplies the review timestamp; these are screening choices, not a definition of a safe pass.

Limits: completed, incomplete and intercepted passes only; sacks and rushing plays are outside this review. The selected receiver may not be the target. Positions do not establish a clear throwing lane, intended read, catch or conversion. Post-release tracking lasts only 0.2–0.5 seconds in this sample. Dotted continuations use straight ground-plane motion; the assumed ±20% ball-speed and ±15° heading spread is an illustrative projection, not a calibrated confidence region. Ball height, landing time, drag and receiver turns are not modeled. Recorded hurry/hit flags apply to the whole play; their timing is unavailable. No flag does not prove a clean pocket. Catches short of the line followed by running gains are outside this screen. Penalties, fumbles and goal-to-go plays are excluded. No practicing-coach validation has been performed.
